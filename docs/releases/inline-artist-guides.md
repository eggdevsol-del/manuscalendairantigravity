# Inline artist guides

Artist first visits and question-mark controls open the contextual guide on the current page. They do not navigate to the isolated practice workspace. The overlay highlights mounted controls and follows tabs, sheets and wizard stages without invoking business actions.

Clicking outside the text box, Close guide, or Escape dismisses the overlay without changing the page, account records, form state or scroll position. Outside pointer events continue to the app normally. Legacy /practice URLs redirect to their originating page (or Today) so persisted links cannot trap the artist in a separate walkthrough.

Required account setup still runs before automatic guide playback. Automatic guide completion is account and page scoped. Existing isolated practice services remain available internally but are no longer artist entry points.
