Promotions: activation & validation

Branch: codex/tattoi-ivory-design-system. Versioned implementation and migrations. Database activation and deployment status must be verified separately.

Implemented

  • Immutable issued terms; artists may edit future issues and archive campaigns without removing issued entitlements.
  • Audience filters use cash receipts less refunds for lifetime paid value, completed sittings for loyalty and past sittings for recency.
  • Client offers in Discover and My Tattoos; use requests notify the artist without prematurely spending credit.
  • Server-calculated deposit and balance quotes. A discount reduces the tattoo price; a voucher contributes prepaid or complimentary credit. Fees apply to the remaining cash collected. Fully covered payments create no fictional cash receipt.
  • Single checkout reservations prevent simultaneous transfer or spending. Cancelling checkout releases the offer; pending provider confirmation prevents premature reuse.
  • Paid gift vouchers, explicit fees, validity beginning at purchase, transfer acceptance/decline/cancel. Cancellation restores sitting credit to its original payer even after the source voucher changes owner.
  • Promotional SMS and push are opt-in. SMS requires verified ownership of a mobile number. Every message has an unsubscribe link. Delivery records distinguish pending, accepted, delivered, failed, skipped and unknown; unknown SMS responses are never blindly replayed.
  • Legacy issued offers migrate with their remaining value and original expiry. Legacy browser-priced redemption is disabled.

Activate on an isolated staging environment

  1. Back up the staging database. Review and run versioned migrations 0028, 0029 and 0030 using the repository migration runner. They have not been run in this task. Do not use automatic schema push.
  2. Set IVORY_OFFERS_ENABLED=true only after migration succeeds. Keep it enabled while issued offers and reserved checkouts exist; disabling this setting also stops offer delivery and cancellation restoration.
  3. Set APP_URL to the staging origin and retain a stable JWT_SECRET. Public payment / unsubscribe links use this origin.
  4. Use Stripe test-mode credentials, connected test artist accounts and webhook delivery to staging. Test payment-intent success, duplicate webhook delivery, cancellation during payment, refunds and balance/deposit races against MySQL before production.
  5. For SMS, supply TWILIO_ACCOUNT_SID, TWILIO_AUTH_TOKEN and TWILIO_MESSAGING_SERVICE_SID as server secrets. Configure an approved sender and use verified test recipient numbers. The worker polls accepted message IDs for delivery status. Credentials alone do not guarantee carrier approval or delivery.
  6. Configure existing VAPID push keys. A client must enable device notifications and separately opt into promotional push. Verify mobile ownership and opt into SMS in Settings → Notifications.
  7. Use fictional artist/client accounts to create an offer, select an audience, verify card visibility, buy/transfer/redeem a voucher, cancel a sitting and inspect restored credit. Check monetary amounts and fees in the ledger.

Validation performed

Full repository suite: 487 passing tests in 110 files. The final suite includes the rounding and first-contact gift-transfer regression tests. TypeScript, frontend/PWA build and server bundle passed. Fixture browser checks: both roles at 320 / 390px, light / dark, no page errors or document overflow. No live database migration or external payment / message integration was exercised.

Operational boundaries

Checkout currently bills in AUD; NZD is not enabled by this feature. Sold vouchers default to at least three years after purchase or no expiry. Non-refundability never overrides statutory rights. Refunds involving already-spent or reserved gift credit require reconciliation rather than automatically clawing money back from clients.

Do not erase offer history or disable the feature as a financial rollback. Preserve the migrations and ledger, and pause campaign issuing instead. Before rollout, reconcile outstanding legacy payment intents; Stripe search may not expose a just-created historical intent immediately.
