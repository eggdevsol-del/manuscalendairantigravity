Activation

Choose a test environment first. Apply migrations 0028_ivory_offers, 0029_offer_lifecycle and 0030_legacy_offers through the versioned migration runner, then set IVORY_OFFERS_ENABLED=true and restart the server. Do not point test clients at production accounts or send campaigns to real clients.

In the app: Artist → Today → Promotions. On the client account, offers appear in My Tattoos and between artist groups in Discover. Apply a booking offer from its deposit or balance review before checkout. Clients can verify their mobile and opt into promotions in Settings → Notifications. Artists can review delivery status and deliberately retry confirmed failures from each campaign.
